# Battlecard Template — Know / Say / Show

One page, sales-facing, verbatim-usable. Default audience is the AE; produce SE/BDR variants only when asked. Every Say claim must have a Show line behind it — claims without evidence don't survive prospect pushback.

```markdown
# Selling against <Competitor>
*Updated <date> · sources in the dossier: outputs/<run>/dossier-<vendor>.md*

## Know — when they show up
- The 2-sentence read: who they are and the deal contexts where they appear
- Early-warning discovery questions (what a prospect says when they're evaluating them)
- Deals to qualify OUT of (from "How we lose" — honesty here is why reps trust the card)

## Say — talk tracks, readable verbatim
- **Quick dismiss** (1 sentence, for when they're a minor player in the deal)
- **How we win** (3 bullets max, each tied to a verified weakness or gap — not feature lists)
- **Landmines to plant** (questions a rep asks that expose their gaps)
- **Objection handling** (when the prospect quotes their pitch: what to say back)

## Show — the evidence behind every Say
- Review counts backing each weakness claim ("support latency: 7 of 20 negative G2 reviews")
- Case studies / named proof points for each win claim
- Pricing intel with fetched-on date

## How we lose (mandatory)
The deals where they beat us and why. A card that says we always win is marketing to ourselves.
```

Rules:
- Natural language a rep can read aloud mid-call — no analyst prose.
- Concise beats comprehensive: cards fail when they try to be encyclopedias.
- Refresh trigger: any fast-decay change (pricing, major release, exec move) same-week, not quarterly.
